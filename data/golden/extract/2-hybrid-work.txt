Subject: Changes to our hybrid working policy

Dear colleagues,

As many of you know, we have spent the last three months reviewing how our hybrid working arrangements are going. Thank you to everyone who filled in the survey; more than eighty percent of you took part, which gave us a very clear picture.

The main message was positive. Most people feel more productive at home, and almost everyone appreciates not having to commute every day. However, a number of concerns came up again and again. New employees in particular said they found it hard to get to know their teams, and several managers reported that important decisions were being delayed because people were rarely in the same room.

With this in mind, we are going to make a few adjustments from the beginning of next month. First, every team will agree on two "anchor days" when everyone is expected to be in the office. Your manager will get in touch with you to arrange this. Second, we will provide a small monthly allowance to cover the cost of equipment and internet at home. Third, the meeting rooms on the second floor are being refurbished so that they are better suited to video calls.

We realise that these changes will not suit everybody. If your personal circumstances make it difficult to come in on the agreed days, please do not hesitate to speak to your manager or to the HR team. We are committed to finding a reasonable solution in each case, and requests will be treated confidentially.

Finally, we would like to emphasise that this is not a step back towards the old way of working. The aim is to keep the flexibility that people value while making sure that nobody feels isolated. We will review the arrangement again in six months and, if necessary, adjust it further.

Best regards,
The People Team
