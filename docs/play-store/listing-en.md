## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Engapp is a flashcard app for Turkish speakers who want English words and phrases to stick. You review for a few minutes a day, and the app schedules each card from your own answers. The app's interface is in Turkish.

SMART REVIEW
• Cards are scheduled with spaced repetition (FSRS): words you struggle with come back often, words you know well come back rarely.
• You set the daily goal and the number of new cards; when reviews pile up, the load is spread over the following days.
• Words you keep forgetting and pairs of words you mix up are flagged.

ADDING WORDS
• Add a word by hand, with your own meaning, example sentence and note.
• Paste an English text you are reading, or upload a PDF: AI picks out the words and phrases for your level as candidates. You see and edit every one before anything is added.
• Import your Anki decks.

ON THE CARDS
• Turkish meaning, part of speech, level (A1–C2) and example sentences.
• Audio for the word and the sentences.
• Memory hints that help a word stick.
• Hide or correct any AI content you think is wrong.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Daily streak and study calendar.
• How well you recall English → Turkish and Turkish → English, and retention by category.
• A 5-minute level test and a weekly summary.

WORKS OFFLINE
• Reviews work without a connection and sync when you are back online.
• Use it as a guest without an account if you like: everything stays on your device.
• Sign in with Google to sync your cards between your phone and your computer.
• Turn on reminders to get at most one review reminder a day.

YOUR DATA
• No ads, no selling of your data, no tracking tools.
• Download all your data as JSON at any time.
• Delete your account and data in the app or on the web.

Good to know: AI features (such as picking words out of a text) need Google sign-in and a connection, and have a daily limit. Text processed with AI is sent to Google Gemini, so do not send confidential or personal text. AI meanings are not always right; check them before adding. The app is for users aged 18 and over.
```
