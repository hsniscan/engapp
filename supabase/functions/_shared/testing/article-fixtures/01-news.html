City council approves new bike lanes after long debate

By Lena Ortiz, Transport correspondent
20 October 2026

After more than four hours of discussion on Tuesday night, the city council voted seven to two in favour of a plan that will add thirty kilometres of protected bike lanes over the next three years.

Supporters say the lanes will make short trips safer and cheaper, especially for students and older residents who do not drive. Opponents argued that the plan would remove too many parking spaces from busy shopping streets.

The first section, along the river, is expected to open next spring. Work on the busier central routes will begin only after a public consultation, which the council promised would include every neighbourhood.

"We listened to people on both sides," said the deputy mayor. "Nobody wants to lose their parking space, but nobody wants another accident outside a school either."

Local shop owners have asked for a delivery zone on each street that loses parking. The council said it would study the request before the second phase begins.
