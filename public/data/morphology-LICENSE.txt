Engapp word-family and morpheme data (morphology-*.json)

This data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International

It is an adaptation of:

- MorphyNet (English derivational morphology, v1), by Khuyagbaatar Batsuren, Gábor Bella and
  Fausto Giunchiglia, https://github.com/kbatsuren/MorphyNet (CC BY-SA 3.0)
- Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org), by the Wiktionary contributors, extracted by kaikki.org /
  wiktextract (Tatu Ylonen) (CC BY-SA 4.0 / GFDL)
- wordfreq 3.1.1, by Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq (data CC BY-SA 4.0)

Changes:
- words limited to the 30,000 most frequent;
- derivations filtered and grouped into families;
- roots and affixes assigned from etymologies;
- Turkish explanations added (written by hand and generated with AI).

Versions and checksums of the sources are listed in data/reference/morphology.sources.json
in the Engapp repository.
